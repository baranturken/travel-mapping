# Design Principles

## Calm first

The app should feel light, clear, and quiet. Avoid visual heaviness and clutter.

## Map is the hero

The map should be the most important visual element once a trip exists.

## Mobile-native simplicity

Tasks should fit a phone screen without feeling cramped or overloaded.

## Mediterranean character

Use brightness, air, and blue-toned accents inspired by sea, sky, and travel.

## Travel confidence

The user should quickly understand where they are going, how they will move, and what comes next.
